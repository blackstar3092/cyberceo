Work with us

Four concrete asks

Speak on a panel

One evening, three segments, and a room of high schoolers who will ask better questions than you expect. Best fit: anyone working in security, from SOC analyst to CISO to federal agent.

Host a site visit

Show a group of students what a real security operation looks like. Nothing sells the field like watching people do it.

Sponsor a season

Workshops are free to every school and residence we visit, which means materials, printing, and travel come out of fundraising. Corporate sponsorships and employer matching go directly into programming.

Open a door

An introduction to a principal, librarian, or residence director is often worth more than a donation. Especially in neighborhoods that don't already have programs like this.
