Collaborations

How to work with us

Almost everything on this site started with one message from a teacher, a librarian, a residence director, or a parent who knew someone. If you want to book a workshop, speak at a panel, sponsor a season, or just point us at someone worth talking to, any of these will reach us.

Contact

Four ways to reach us

Email cyberceo.dnhs@gmail.com The fastest way to reach us, and the best option for booking a workshop. Tell us who we would be speaking to and roughly how long we have. LinkedIn linkedin.com/company/cyberceo-sd Best for professionals and companies. Message us here about speaking on a panel, hosting a site visit, or sponsorship. Instagram @dnhs_cyberceo Event photos, updates, and notice of upcoming workshops. Our DMs are open if that is easier than email.
In person Del Norte High School Ask for Mr. Mortensen, our faculty advisor. Del Norte students and staff can also just find us at a Monday meeting.

Ways to help

What we are actually asking for

Speak on a panel

One evening, three segments, and a room of high schoolers who ask better questions than you would expect. We are looking for anyone working in security, from analysts to engineers to federal agents. You do not need to prepare slides.

Host a site visit

Show a group of our members what a real security operation looks like. Nothing sells the field to a teenager like watching people do the job.

Sponsor a season

Every workshop is free to the school or residence hosting it, so materials, printing, and travel come out of fundraising. Corporate sponsorships and employer matching go directly into programming.

Make an introduction

An introduction to a principal, librarian, or residence director is often worth more to us than a donation, particularly in districts and neighborhoods that do not already have programs like this.

Expert panel

Who joined us at Del Norte

Our first Cybersecurity Expert Panel brought three professionals to Del Norte to talk about the threat landscape, career paths, and where the field is heading. We are running a second panel this year.

David Tomasz

Intelligence Analyst, FBI San Diego Field Office

Works in the FBI's San Diego cybercriminal squad, supporting investigations across San Diego and Imperial County and studying emerging cybercriminal threats. He is a ransomware subject matter expert whose papers have been briefed to the president and the national security council. Before the Bureau he was Director of Research and Development at the Southeast Asia Center and taught international relations and comparative politics at Texas State University.

Tricia Mercer

Chief People Officer, INDUS

Leads talent, human resources, security, facilities, IT, and administration at INDUS. She has over 20 years of defense industry experience and supported C4I systems as an information systems and cybersecurity engineer before moving into leadership. She also serves on the boards of the San Diego Cyber Center of Excellence and United Way of San Diego County.

Victor Nzeata

Founder and CEO, Cyber Brain Academy

In 2016 he became the U.S. Army Reserve's first graduate of the U.S. Army Cyber School, and went on to lead teams in defensive and offensive cyber operations. He now runs Cyber Brain Academy, which provides cybersecurity training and certification preparation, teaches as an adjunct professor at the University of San Diego, and is pursuing a Ph.D. in information security at Purdue.

The panel runs in three segments, the threat landscape, career opportunities, and the future of the field, with moderated and student submitted questions throughout.
