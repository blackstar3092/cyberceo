Structure

How the work divides

Three subteams, each owning a stage of the process. A lesson is written by Curriculum, rehearsed and delivered by Instructional, and given an audience by Marketing.

Curriculum
Writes and revises the lesson decks, the games, and the Teaching Cyber Safety guide.
Instructional
Rehearses and delivers workshops on site. The members presenting at the front of the room.
Marketing
Social media, event promotion, the enrollment flyer, and partner communications.
