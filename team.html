Team

Twenty-five students, three subteams, one Monday a week.

Structure

How the work divides.

Three subteams, each owning a stage of the pipeline. A lesson is written by Curriculum, rehearsed and delivered by Instructional, and filled with an audience by Marketing.

Curriculum
Writes and revises the lesson decks, games, and the Teaching Cyber Safety guide.
Instructional
Rehearses and delivers workshops on site. The students at the front of the room.
Marketing
Social media, event promotion, the enrollment flyer, and partner communications.
