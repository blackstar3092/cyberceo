Del Norte High School · Established Summer 2023

Free cybersecurity education for schools and senior communities

CyberCEO is a student-run outreach nonprofit based at Del Norte High School. We run workshops for elementary students, training for the staff who support senior residents, and career panels for high schoolers. Every session is free, and we bring our own materials.

Why it matters

Most people never learn cybersecurity essentials until too late.

Elementary students are often exposed to threat actors before they understand digital safety. Senior residents are one of the most targeted groups for phone and email fraud, and the staff who support them rarely get training on how to prevent it.

We open every assembly with three real incidents so that students understand the subject is not hypothetical before we get into passwords and phishing.

WannaCry $4 billion

Ransomware that spread worldwide, locking hospitals and businesses out of their own files.

CrowdStrike outage $10 billion

One bad software update grounded flights and froze systems globally. Not every major incident is an attack.

Stuxnet Physical damage

Code that broke real machinery and changed how governments think about cyberweapons.

What we do

Three programs, three audiences

Elementary assemblies

An Introduction to Cybersecurity workshop for fourth and fifth graders. We cover what cybersecurity is, how to build a password that holds up, and how to recognize a scam email, using games and live demos rather than lecturing.

See the lesson

Senior residences

We train the caretakers and staff who support senior residents, then leave behind a written guide covering how to protect accounts and what to do if a resident has already been targeted.

See the guide

High school panels

Professionals from the FBI, the defense industry, and cybersecurity training companies come to Del Norte to talk about the threat landscape, career paths, and where the field is heading.

2026 / 27 focus

Expansion

Last year we set out to diversify what we offer, and we got through it: new lesson formats, sessions at senior community events, and our first school outside Poway Unified. This year is about running those same programs in more places.

More senior residences

Returning to Villa Rancho Bernardo and adding homes such as Remington House, with staff training and the caretaker guide at each one.

More districts

Continuing past Poway Unified into elementary schools in districts we have not worked with before, with a focus on schools that have no program like this.

More panels and camps

Another Cybersecurity Expert Panel at Del Norte, plus library workshops and summer camps that anyone can sign up for.

Get involved

There are four ways to work with us

Most of what we have done started with one email from a teacher, a librarian, or a parent who knew someone. If any of these describe you, get in touch.

Schools, libraries, senior residences

Book a workshop

Assemblies fit a standard class period. Staff training runs about an hour. Both are free, and we supply the slides, games, and handouts. Tell us who we would be speaking to and how long we have.

Request a workshop

Del Norte students

Join the club

We meet Mondays at Del Norte during the school year and online over the summer. You do not need any background in cybersecurity. Members write lessons, present at workshops, and run outreach.

How the team works

Industry and public sector

Speak, host, or sponsor

Join a panel, host a site visit for our members, or sponsor a season of programming. Sponsorships and employer matching go straight into materials, printing, and travel, which is what keeps workshops free.
