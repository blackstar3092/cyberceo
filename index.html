Why it matters

We open every assembly with the receipts.

Fourth graders don't need to be scared — they need to understand that the thing they're learning is real, and expensive. These are the three cases we walk through before we ever mention a password.

WannaCry $4 billion

Ransomware that spread worldwide, locking hospitals and businesses out of their own files.

CrowdStrike outage $10 billion

One bad update grounded flights and froze systems globally — a reminder that not every incident is an attack.

Stuxnet Physical damage

Code that broke real machinery, and changed what governments think cyberweapons can do.
